For Corporations • Attire Consultancy + Customization

Your Enterprise Doesn't Need Uniforms.
It Needs a Walking Brand Symbol.

What an enterprise needs is not a set of clothes, but a team with brand recognition and a sense of ritual. HARCHOY works as your corporate attire management consultant: we define the attire strategy from your brand first, then design, produce and deliver the garments that carry it.

Our Method

Three Questions Before a Single Sketch

Most uniform programs fail because they start from a catalog. Ours start from three questions that define the real problem of corporate attire.

Question 01 • Competition

What does your industry look like?

We map the visual landscape of your competitive environment — what your peers' teams wear, what conventions exist, and where your brand can visibly stand apart without breaking professional expectations.

Question 02 • Scenarios

Who wears it, and where?

We research the actual wearers and wearing scenes: front desk or field work, client-facing or backstage, climate, movement, dress code. Attire must perform in the real day of the people wearing it.

Question 03 • Brand Energy

Who sees it, and what should they feel?

Attire is worn to be seen — by customers, partners, and your own team. We define the brand message the clothing must carry, so every team member becomes a consistent, recognizable point of your brand.
