Luxury Hospitality

Luxelakes International Community — Chengdu, China

200 bespoke suits. DDP delivery to Chengdu. Zero hassle. Savile Row-level fit at a fraction of the cost.

The Challenge

Luxelakes needed 200 bespoke suits for their executive team — but their procurement team had never sourced bespoke manufacturing overseas. They worried about:

  • Quality control without physical access to the factory
  • DDP shipping risk (customs, duties, last-mile delivery)
  • Fit consistency across 200 different body measurements

The HARCHOY Solution

We delivered a complete turnkey program:

  • Savile Street Measurement System — 25+ dimensions per executive, with posture correction and smart ease allowance
  • DDP to Chengdu — customs, duties, inland logistics all handled by HARCHOY. Client received goods at their doorstep, zero hidden costs
  • 3-round sample protocol — first sample for fabric/colour confirmation, second for fit verification, production batch only after sign-off

The Result

200 Suits Delivered
0 Quality Complaints
DDP Delivery Mode

From first sample to final delivery in 6 weeks. The Luxelakes procurement director called it “the smoothest overseas sourcing experience we’ve ever had.”

Bespoke Studio Partner

Susie Jenny — Sydney, Australia

$51,625 single order. The benchmark that proves HARCHOY can deliver at scale for bespoke studio partners.

The Partnership

Susie Jenny operates a high-end bespoke studio in Sydney’s Paddington district, serving C-suite executives, celebrities, and discerning professionals. She needed:

  • A manufacturer capable of matching her studio’s exacting standards
  • Consistent quality across repeated orders (not one-off magic)
  • DDP shipping so her clients never see logistics friction

How HARCHOY Delivered

Over 12 months, HARCHOY became Susie’s invisible manufacturing partner:

  • Measurement-to-pattern pipeline — Susie’s client measurements fed directly into our Savile Street system, eliminating re-measurement errors
  • 3-round sample protocol — every new client gets 3 sampling rounds before production; repeat clients skip to production after one fit confirmation
  • Quality guarantee — any suit that doesn’t pass Susie’s final inspection is remade at no charge, no questions asked

The Numbers

$51,625 Single Order Value
12 Months Partnership
100% Repeat Order Rate

The $51,625 order is HARCHOY’s benchmark for what a true bespoke partnership looks like — not a transaction, but a long-term, zero-friction manufacturing alliance.

Enterprise Uniform

Middle East Corporate Client — 500 Suits

500 bespoke uniforms. 3-tier hierarchy. Single DDP shipment. 8-week delivery window.

The Challenge

A Middle East corporate client needed 500 bespoke suits across three management tiers (Executive, Senior, Standard). Challenges:

  • Three distinct design specs with different fabric/trim requirements
  • 500 individual body measurements — no batch averaging
  • Single shipment window — all 500 suits arrive together

The HARCHOY Solution

We built a dedicated production line for this program:

  • Tier-specific production tracks — three parallel workflows, each with its own QA checkpoint
  • Smart ease allowance — executives get structured ease; standard tier gets comfort ease
  • Container-optimised DDP — all 500 suits packed in one container, client receives at their warehouse

The Result

500 Suits Delivered
3 Design Tiers
DDP Delivery Mode

8 weeks from measurement collection to warehouse delivery. The client’s HR director noted: “This is the first time our uniform program didn’t require a single follow-up email.”
